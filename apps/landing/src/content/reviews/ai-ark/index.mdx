## What is AI Ark?

AI Ark is a B2B data platform built on a database of more than 400 million
people and 70 million companies. It finds people and companies through a web
app and an API, and returns work emails and mobile numbers for them.

Its pitch is paying only for what comes back. An email lookup costs one
credit when AI Ark finds an address and nothing when it doesn't, and every
address is verified with BounceBan at the moment it is returned.

## Mid-pack coverage

We ran AI Ark on the same 120 LinkedIn profiles as ten other providers. It
found a work email for 50 of them, or 42%, which puts it in the middle of
the field: Surfe found 69% and Wiza 66%, while Blitz found 38% and Hunter
19%. On the next 111 profiles it found 45%.

Its data accuracy was below average. Across all 228 profiles it looked up, AI
Ark found slightly more than Blitz on the same people, 43.9% against 41.7%.

For comparison, pipe0's default work email waterfall found an email for 101
of the same 120 people, 84%. AI Ark found one email that waterfall missed,
and none that all ten other providers missed.

## Seconds, not milliseconds

AI Ark checks each email while you wait, and that shows in the response
time. Lookups took a median of 6 seconds across all 228 profiles, and the
slowest tenth took 31 seconds or more. Lookups that found nothing were the
slowest, at a median of 16 seconds, against 4 seconds for those that found
an email.

That is fine for a batch job, but slow when an agent or a live form is
waiting on each lookup. Blitz answered the same kind of lookup in 0.3
seconds.

The free plan adds its own limit. Our free key allowed 5 requests a minute,
not the 5 per second the docs describe, so a test of a few hundred profiles
takes hours.

## What it costs

There is a free plan with 100 credits a month and a $49 plan with 5,000
credits a month. AI Ark says plans above 30,000 credits a month cost $2 to
$4 per 1,000 credits. A verified email costs one credit and a mobile number
five, both charged only when one is found.

That makes AI Ark one of the cheapest ways to buy an email: about a cent each
on the $49 plan. Because misses are free, the cost per email found is the
same as the cost per lookup. People search and reverse email lookup are
billed differently, per result returned and per request.

## AI Ark and pipe0

pipe0 competes with AI Ark and doesn't use it in its waterfalls. We tested
it on the same inputs as every other provider in our benchmark.

Because AI Ark only charges for emails it finds, it can sit in front of a
waterfall without wasting money on misses. In our test, running AI Ark first
and sending only the people it missed through pipe0 found 102 of 120, one
more than pipe0 alone, and cut pipe0 spend by about 40%. The 50 emails AI
Ark found cost about 50 cents.

## Who should buy AI Ark

Buy AI Ark if you want verified emails at about a cent each, pay only for
what you get, and run your lookups in batches.

Look elsewhere if you need an email for as many people on your list as
possible, or if an agent or a live form waits on each lookup.
