## What is Amplemarket?

Amplemarket is a sales platform. Reps use it to find prospects, write and
send multichannel sequences, call from a built-in dialer and work replies
in one inbox, with AI agents it calls Duo doing much of the research and
writing. João Batalha and his co-founders launched it at the end of 2019,
and it raised $12 million in 2022.

Under all of that sits its own people and company data: work emails,
mobile numbers, LinkedIn profiles, firmographics and tech stacks. That data
is what this review covers. We didn't test the sequences, the inbox, the
dialer or the AI agents, and the scores say nothing about them.

## How good is Amplemarket's data?

Amplemarket's data accuracy is above average. We ran the same LinkedIn
profiles through nine providers, and Amplemarket found a mobile number for
119 of 150, second only to Surfe.

It was second again on the hardest test we run, finding the LinkedIn
profile behind a signup email address, and its answers there agreed with
the other providers 94% of the time. On work emails it found an address
for 56% of 120 profiles, fourth of nine, and answered in half a second.

Its company data is broad too. On 20 websites it found a CRM, payment,
email or pixel tool in as many categories as BuiltWith, plus back-office
software a website doesn't show.

The list has no detection dates, so a shop platform a store left years ago
looks the same as the one it runs today. Check before you filter on it.

## Where it falls short

Price, for anyone who only wants the data. Amplemarket doesn't sell data on
its own: you buy a sales platform, from $600 a month on an annual term for
two users. Startup includes 600 phone credits per user a year, about 50 a
month. If your team already lives in Amplemarket, the data comes with it.
If you just need enrichment, you pay for a lot of software you won't use.

Speed on phone numbers is the other. Mobile lookups took a median of 18.6
seconds per person in our test. That is fine for a list, not for a live
product.

## Amplemarket for developers and agents

The API is on every account: create a key under Settings > API. One
request finds a person by LinkedIn URL, email, or name and company, and
reveals their email and phone number if you ask. Batches take up to 10,000
people or companies, and the limit is 500 requests a minute. Amplemarket
also lists an MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT on its pricing page.

## Amplemarket and pipe0

We buy data from Amplemarket. It runs second in pipe0's default work email
waterfall, after Wiza, and first in the waterfalls that find people by job
title and look up a company's funding. Its company data and job openings
are pipe0 pipes of their own. We put it there because of the benchmark
above, and the scores on this page come from the same numbers.

If you want Amplemarket's data without a platform contract, pipe0 is one
way to get it: you pay per result, and Amplemarket sits inside waterfalls
you can see and reorder.

## Who should buy Amplemarket

Buy Amplemarket if you want prospecting data and outreach in one platform,
and your reps will use both. The data inside it is among the most reliable
we have measured.

Look elsewhere if you only need data. [Surfe](/reviews/surfe) has higher
mobile coverage on per-user plans, and an API waterfall gives you
Amplemarket's data next to other sources, billed per result.
