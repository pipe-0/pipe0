## What is Fiber AI?

Fiber AI is a data API for people and companies, built in New York by Adi
Agashe and Neel Mehta after Y Combinator's summer 2023 batch. It does the
things a GTM agent needs from one key: search people and companies, enrich a
profile, reveal emails and phone numbers, fetch a LinkedIn profile live,
track job changes, and watch companies for signals. It says it covers 850
million people and 40 million companies.

What sets it apart is how much of it is written for software. Every
operation has its own markdown page for agents, the OpenAPI spec is public,
and every response says what the call cost.

## Where its data is strongest

We ran Fiber through the same tests as every other provider, and its
results are uneven in an interesting way: excellent on email, average on
phones.

The standout is the reverse lookup. Given 150 email addresses from real
pipe0 signups, Fiber found the person's LinkedIn profile for 38% of them.
The next best provider in that run found 25%. That gap is large enough to
survive the small sample, and Fiber's answers matched the other providers
92% of the time when both found someone.

On work email from a LinkedIn profile, Fiber found 76% of 50, the highest
of the ten providers in that run. On personal email it found 51% of 150,
within a point of the two leaders. Mobile numbers are where it falls back
to the middle: 65% of 150 profiles, well behind the 96% [Surfe](/reviews/surfe)
reached on the same records.

Fiber also sells a premium phone product at 5 credits instead of 3. On the
same 50 profiles, it found 64% of mobile numbers against 66% for the
standard reveal. Save the credits.

## What it costs

A credit is 2 cents on a subscription. A work or personal email is 2
credits, so 4 cents; a phone number is 3, so 6 cents; and a reveal that
finds nothing is free. Those are low prices for the coverage. Embedded
waterfalls in other tools often charge 8 to 30 cents per email.

The catch is the entry point. The smallest plan is $250 a month billed
annually for 15,000 credits, and there is no free plan, only a trial of 500
credits with a card or 100 through the API without one. A team that needs a
few hundred lookups a month will pay for capacity it doesn't use.

## Built for agents

Fiber runs four MCP servers, each for a different situation: a core server
that discovers and calls any of its 250-odd operations, one with direct
tools for common jobs, one with OAuth for Claude and ChatGPT, and a lite
version. It is listed in the official MCP registry and ships a Claude Code
plugin.

Rate limits are generous where it matters: 3,500 reverse lookups a minute
and 200 contact reveals. Long jobs run asynchronously with signed webhooks.
The one thing to plan around is speed on phones, where a reveal can take
ten seconds or more, and Fiber itself recommends a two-minute timeout.

## Fiber AI and pipe0

pipe0 doesn't use Fiber today. We added it to our benchmark as a candidate
for our waterfalls, and the numbers above are from that work. Its reverse
lookup is the strongest result we have for that path.

## Who should buy Fiber AI

Buy Fiber if you are building an agent or a product that needs people data
and contact details, especially if you start from email addresses. The
price per lookup is low and the tooling is among the best we have seen.

Look elsewhere if you need a free plan, if your volume is small, or if phone
numbers are the main thing you buy. For mobiles, Surfe found the most in our
tests; for company signals, [Crustdata](/reviews/crustdata) goes deeper.
