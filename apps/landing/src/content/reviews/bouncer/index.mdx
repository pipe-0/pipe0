## What is Bouncer?

Bouncer checks whether an email address will accept mail before you send to
it. It started in 2017 in Wroclaw, Poland, keeps its data in EU data centers,
and sells mostly to marketing and outbound teams through list uploads, an
API, and about 25 integrations, from HubSpot and Mailchimp to lemlist and
Make.

Each check returns one of four statuses (deliverable, risky, undeliverable,
or unknown) with a reason and a 0 to 100 score. Around verification, Bouncer
sells a deliverability kit, a signup form widget, and automatic cleaning of
CRM contacts.

## How it did in our test

We ran 100 labelled addresses through six verifiers. Fifty were real: pipe0
users who had signed in with Google in the previous 45 days, 30 on work
domains and 20 on Gmail. Fifty were fake: random mailboxes at Gmail, Outlook,
and well-known company domains, and addresses at domains that don't exist.

Bouncer got 93 right, tied with MixRank and behind LeadMagic (97) and
ZeroBounce (96). It confirmed 47 of the 50 real addresses and caught 46 of
the 50 fakes.

Its strength is work domains. Bouncer flagged 17 of our 30 real work domains
as accept-all, the kind of server that takes mail for any address, and
still confirmed 16 of those addresses as deliverable. MillionVerifier and
Emailable returned catch-all for most of the same addresses, which leaves
the decision to you.

Its weak spot is one answer. A random mailbox we made up at hubspot.com came
back deliverable with a perfect score of 100. LeadMagic, ZeroBounce, MixRank,
and MillionVerifier all rejected it. Sending only to Bouncer's valid results
would have bounced once in our test; with ZeroBounce or LeadMagic, never.

It also rejected two real addresses, one of which every verifier rejected,
and marked one real Gmail address risky where every other verifier said
valid.

## What it costs

Pay-as-you-go starts at $8 for 1,000 checks and falls to $60 for 10,000 and
$2,000 for a million. That is about half of ZeroBounce ($129 for 10,000) and
more than MillionVerifier ($39). A monthly subscription takes 20% off.

Credits never expire, and unknown results and duplicates are free. New
accounts get 100 credits.

## Bouncer for developers and agents

The real-time API takes one GET per address and allows 1,000 requests a
minute. It answered in a median of half a second in our test, with nothing
left unknown. A batch API takes up to 500,000 addresses per job. The only
official SDK is for Java, so most teams call the REST API directly.

Bouncer runs an official MCP server at `api.usebouncer.com/mcp` with OAuth
sign-in, documented for Claude and ChatGPT, so an agent can verify an address
before it writes it to a CRM or a sequence.

## Who should buy Bouncer

Buy Bouncer if you send to work addresses and want answers on accept-all
domains without paying ZeroBounce prices, especially if you buy credits in
bulk and use them slowly. It is the best value of the verifiers we tested on
work domains.

Pick [ZeroBounce](/reviews/zerobounce) or [LeadMagic](/reviews/leadmagic)
instead if a single fake passed as valid costs you more than the price
difference, or if you need SOC 2 paperwork. For consumer lists,
[MillionVerifier](/reviews/millionverifier) is just as accurate and
cheaper.
