## What is Forager?

Forager is B2B data infrastructure: people, company, and job data for
platforms, AI agents, and waterfall tools rather than for salespeople
clicking through an app. It says it holds more than 850 million person and
company records and 200 million mobile numbers, and it recrawls them about
every 2.5 weeks. You can query it by API, or take the whole dataset as a feed
into S3, Google Cloud Storage, or Snowflake.

## Speed first

Forager's defining number in our benchmark is speed. It answered phone
lookups from LinkedIn profiles in a median of 80 milliseconds. Wiza took 3
seconds on the same profiles and Amplemarket 18.6. For a product where a
user is waiting, or an agent making hundreds of lookups in a row, that
difference is the whole experience.

Speed doesn't come at the cost of accuracy. When Forager and Amplemarket both
found a mobile number for the same person, they matched 50 times out of 58,
and with Surfe 56 out of 65. Its coverage sits in the middle: it found a
mobile for 43% of 150 profiles, behind the providers that run their own
waterfalls.

On personal email it was among the best. From the same set of profiles,
Forager and Wiza tied for the most consumer addresses found, at 52% each.
Its personal email endpoint only returns webmail addresses like Gmail and
Outlook, which is usually what a personal email search is for anyway.

## What it costs

Plans start at $50 a month for 2,250 credits, then $100 for 5,250 and $250
for 16,200, with 10% off billed annually. A phone number costs 15 credits and
an email 5, which Forager itself shows as 21 to 33 cents per mobile and 7 to 11
cents per email. Monthly credits roll over and plans cancel anytime.

The docs leave some questions open. Email lookups that find nothing are
free, but the docs don't say whether the same is true for phones, and rate
limits aren't published.

## Forager for developers

Most endpoints are synchronous and very fast; work email lookups run as
async jobs you poll, with a webhook for bulk requests. There is no MCP
server, so agents reach Forager through its REST API or through a tool that
wraps it.

## Forager and pipe0

We buy data from Forager. It is part of pipe0's default personal email
waterfall, chosen for the personal email result above, and its speed makes
it a good fit wherever an agent is waiting on the answer.

## Who should buy Forager

Buy Forager if you are building a product or an agent that needs contact
data in real time, or if you want bulk people and company data delivered as
a feed.

Look elsewhere if you want an MCP server, a prospecting app for your sales
team, or the highest mobile coverage from a single provider.
