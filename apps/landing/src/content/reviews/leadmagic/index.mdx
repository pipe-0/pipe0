## What is LeadMagic?

LeadMagic is a contact data API. Jesse Ouellette launched it in February
2024 after years of building data workflows for clients, and runs it
without venture money from Needham, Massachusetts. One key covers work and
personal emails, mobile numbers, LinkedIn profile enrichment, job change
detection, company funding, technographics, and ad library search.

It has about 2,000 active subscribers, and many of them never need its web
app: they call it from Clay, where it is a native provider, from n8n or Make,
from their own code, or from an agent over MCP.

## Where it fits

LeadMagic's data has a clear shape: when it answers, it is usually right,
and it answers less often than the premium sources. In our name and domain
test on 100 real pipe0 signups, it found a work email for half of them, and
those addresses matched other providers 95% of the time. Against
Amplemarket, a premium source, it agreed on 21 of 22 people.

That shape is exactly what the first slot of a waterfall needs. A waterfall
stops at the first answer, so the first provider decides accuracy for every
person it answers, and its price is paid on every lookup. A cheap provider
that is right when it answers leaves only the remaining people for the
expensive providers behind it.

## What it costs

LeadMagic is among the cheapest contact data we buy. Credits cost about 2
cents on the smallest plan and 0.7 cents on the largest, a work email is 1
credit, and an empty lookup is free. Monthly plans let unused credits pile up to
twice the monthly amount. Professional and Ultimate also make the search API
free of credits.

There is no free trial; the smallest plan is $83 a month billed annually.
One billing detail to watch: the job change detector costs 3 credits per
person checked, even when nothing changed.

## LeadMagic for agents

Every plan includes the API, a command-line tool, and an MCP server with
more than 130 tools, which works in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, and
about a dozen other clients over OAuth. Email finding allows 600 requests a
minute, which is plenty for an agent and enough for most batch jobs.

## LeadMagic and pipe0

We buy data from LeadMagic, and it runs first in several pipe0 waterfalls,
including name and domain to work email and email to LinkedIn profile. We
put it there for the reasons above: low price and answers that agree with
premium sources. The providers behind it pick up the people it doesn't
find.

## Who should buy LeadMagic

Buy LeadMagic if you enrich at volume, care about cost per result, and can
put a second provider behind it for the people it misses.

Look elsewhere if you want the most coverage from a single provider. For
work emails from names and domains, [Hunter](/reviews/hunter) found more in
our benchmark; for mobile numbers, [Surfe](/reviews/surfe) did.
