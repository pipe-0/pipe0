## What is Wiza?

Wiza started as the tool that turns a LinkedIn Sales Navigator search into a
spreadsheet of emails, and that is still how most of its 50,000-plus business
customers use it. Export a search, a lead list, or a Recruiter project, or
open a profile with the Chrome extension, and Wiza adds work emails, personal
emails, and up to three mobile numbers per person.

Underneath is an API that does the same from a LinkedIn URL, a name and a
company domain, or an email address, plus an MCP server for agents.

## Consistent where others aren't

Most contact providers are good at one input and weak at another. Wiza was
strong on most of them in our benchmark. Given the names and company
domains of 100 real pipe0 signups, it found a work email for 73. From 120
LinkedIn profiles it found 66% of work emails, and from 150 profiles it found
65% of mobile numbers. On personal email, it tied for first at 52%.

That consistency is why it runs first in pipe0's default mobile and work
email waterfalls. When Wiza and Fiber both found a mobile number for the
same person, they matched 91 times out of 94.

## Speed: fast phones, slow emails

Wiza verifies emails in real time instead of serving them from a cache, and
that costs time. Its own docs put an average work email reveal at about 25
seconds, sometimes several minutes. Phone numbers are a different story: in
our test they came back in a median of 3 seconds, faster than Surfe at 4.4
seconds and Amplemarket at 18.6.

The API is asynchronous for that reason. You start a reveal and either poll
for the result or give Wiza a webhook to call.

## What it costs

There are two ways to pay. App plans are per user: a free plan with 20
emails and 5 phones, Starter at $49 a user per month, and Email + Phone at
$199, or $166 billed annually for unlimited reveals with 2,500 bulk exports a
month. Monthly credits reset on the billing date.

The API has its own credits at 2.5 cents each. An email costs 2 credits and a
phone 5, so about 5 and 12.5 cents, charged only when Wiza finds something.
For a developer or an agent, that is one of the lower prices in the market.

## Wiza and pipe0

We buy data from Wiza. It runs first in pipe0's default mobile and work
email waterfalls, chosen on price and the coverage above, with Surfe and
Crustdata behind it for the people it misses.

## Who should buy Wiza

Buy Wiza if your team prospects in Sales Navigator, or if you need one API
that finds emails, personal emails, and phones from whatever input you have.

Look elsewhere if you need an email back in under a second, or if a large
team needs data but only a few people would use a seat.
