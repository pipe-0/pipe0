## What is Hunter?

Hunter has been finding email addresses since 2015, when Antoine Finkelstein
and François Grante built a tool that answers one question: what is the
email address of this person at this company? Type a domain and Hunter lists
the people it knows there with their addresses. Type a name and a domain and
it returns the most likely address, a confidence score, and the web pages
where it found it.

Around that it has added a verifier, a company search, cold email sequences,
a browser extension, a Google Sheets add-on, an API, and an MCP server. More
than seven million accounts have signed up, and every plan, including the
free one, comes with unlimited users.

## Why it wins on name and domain

Most providers start from a LinkedIn profile. Hunter starts from the
company's website, and that is exactly what a name-and-domain lookup needs.
It crawls about 30 million pages a day and indexes the addresses it finds,
so it knows each company's email pattern from real examples, not from
assumptions.

In our benchmark, we gave five providers the names and company domains of
100 real pipe0 signups. Hunter found a work email for 77 of them. The next
best found 50. When we repeated the test in September with Wiza added,
Hunter found 77% again and Wiza 73%. Its answers also held up: when Hunter
and another provider both found an address, they matched 95% of the time.

Hunter is honest about where an address comes from. Each result shows its
public sources, or says it was derived from the company's pattern. Addresses
that no longer appear anywhere are removed after six months.

## What it costs

The free plan gives 50 credits a month. Starter costs $34 a month billed
annually for 2,000 credits, which is under 2 cents per email found, and a
lookup that finds nothing is free. Repeating a search in the same billing
period costs nothing either, and verification is half a credit. Visitors in
Europe see the same figures in euros.

Monthly credits don't roll over. On yearly plans, you get the year's credits
up front, which helps teams whose volume comes in bursts.

## Hunter for agents

Hunter's API and MCP server are available on every plan, the free one
included. The API allows 15 requests a second for searches, and the MCP
server at `mcp.hunter.io` works with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and others,
with OAuth where the client supports it. Every email comes back with a
confidence score, which is what an agent needs to decide whether to use it.

## Hunter and pipe0

We buy data from Hunter. It is part of pipe0's name-and-domain work email
waterfall, and its benchmark result is why it stays there. Its strength is
specific: give it a name and a domain and it does better than anyone else
we have tested. Starting from a LinkedIn profile, and for phone numbers,
other providers take over.

## Who should buy Hunter

Buy Hunter if you know who you want to reach and where they work, and you
need their work email. The free plan is enough to judge it, and the paid
plans are cheap per address.

Look elsewhere if you need mobile numbers or start from LinkedIn profiles.
[LeadMagic](/reviews/leadmagic) covers emails and phones from one API, and
[Surfe](/reviews/surfe) found the most mobile numbers in our tests.
