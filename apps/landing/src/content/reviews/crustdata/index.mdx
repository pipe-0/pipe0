## What is Crustdata?

Crustdata is a B2B data API, and it was designed for software to read rather
than for people to click. You give it a company or a person and get back a
structured record: for a company, headcount and how it is trending, funding
rounds, open jobs, web traffic and where it comes from, the tech stack,
news, reviews, and the leadership team; for a person, their profile, career,
and on Enterprise plans their email and phone number.

Abhilash Chowdhary, Manmohit Grewal, and Chris Pisarski built it in San
Francisco and went through Y Combinator in fall 2024. It raised a $6 million
seed in October 2025, and its customers include YC itself.

## Company data that stays current

The difference between Crustdata and most data providers is what a record
contains and how old it is. A company lookup does not stop at industry and
size. It shows whether headcount grew over the last six months, which roles
the company is hiring for and where, and whether its web traffic jumped
after a launch. Those are the facts outbound actually runs on.

Its dataset refreshes every 14 to 28 days. When that is too old, live
endpoints fetch a person or company at request time. Watchers go one step
further: you register the companies or people you care about, and Crustdata
sends a webhook when something changes, such as a new job posting, a
funding round, or a leadership move. For signal-based outbound, that
replaces a pile of scrapers.

## Contact data

Crustdata also sells emails and phone numbers, through a contact endpoint on
Enterprise plans. We benchmarked it: across 150 LinkedIn profiles, Crustdata
found a mobile number for 78%, within two points of the best single premium
source we tested. It was also the fastest of the high-coverage providers,
with a median response of 0.9 seconds against 4.4 and 18.6 seconds for the
other two. Speed like that matters when an agent is waiting on the answer.

## Built for agents

Crustdata describes itself as a data provider for AI agents, and the tooling
backs that up. The MCP server installs in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code,
and Codex and signs in with OAuth. A dry-run mode shows what a call would
cost before it spends anything, which is exactly what you want when an agent
is deciding what to look up. The docs publish an `llms.txt` index, and your
account's own prices can be read from the API for free.

The limits are on throughput. By default, enrichment allows 15 requests a
minute and search 30, and contact data needs an Enterprise plan. Larger
jobs go through batch endpoints that run asynchronously. For an agent doing
account research, that is plenty; for bulk enrichment of a million rows, talk
to Crustdata first.

## Crustdata and pipe0

We buy data from Crustdata. It runs in pipe0's default mobile and work email
waterfalls, and it powers pipe0's people and company searches, job search,
and company matching. We chose it for the freshness and depth of its company
data and for the speed of its contact lookups.

If you want Crustdata's data without a separate contract, pipe0 is one way
to get it: the same searches and enrichments sit next to 50+ other
providers, behind one API and MCP server, billed per result.

## Who should buy Crustdata

Buy Crustdata if you are building an agent or a product that needs current
company and people data, or if your outbound runs on signals like hiring,
headcount, and funding. It is the most agent-ready B2B data API we have
worked with.

Look elsewhere if your team wants a prospecting app with a UI, or if you
only need contact details. For mobile numbers at the highest coverage,
[Surfe](/reviews/surfe) led our benchmark.
