## What is Prospeo?

Prospeo is a contact data tool from Toronto, founded in 2023, that has grown
quickly on one pitch: verified work emails and mobile numbers, cheaply. You
can search people and companies with 30-odd filters, enrich a CSV, use a
Chrome extension on LinkedIn or any company website, or call its API. It
says more than 15,000 companies use it.

## Fast and accurate, not exhaustive

Prospeo's data has a clear profile in our benchmark. It doesn't find the
most people: from 120 LinkedIn profiles it found 46% of work emails, and from
150 profiles 33% of mobile numbers. But what it finds matches the premium
providers. When Prospeo and Surfe both found a mobile number for the same
person, they matched 47 times out of 48. On reverse email lookups, where it
found the second most profiles in that run, its answers agreed with other
providers 90% of the time.

And it is fast. Prospeo answered work email lookups in a median of 0.2
seconds, against 3.6 seconds for Amplemarket and 14.8 for Surfe on the same
records.

That combination is exactly what the first slot of a waterfall needs. The
first provider is asked about everyone, so it has to be cheap and quick, and
whatever it answers is final, so it has to be right. A provider like
Prospeo answers the easy half of a list in a fraction of a second and leaves
the rest to slower, broader sources.

## What it costs

There is a free plan with 100 credits a month. Starter costs $49 a month for
2,000 credits, Growth $99 for 5,000, and Pro $249 for 15,000, all per user and
about 25% cheaper billed annually. A verified email costs 1 credit, about 2
cents on Growth, and a mobile number 10, with the email included. Misses are
free, and asking about the same person again within 90 days is free too.

## Prospeo for developers and agents

The API is synchronous, so a lookup returns its answer in the same call,
with bulk endpoints for 50 records at a time. The MCP server at
`mcp.prospeo.io` signs in with OAuth or an API key, and there is also a
local npm package. Rate limits are modest on the smaller plans, 5 requests a
second for enrichment, and rise sharply on Pro.

## Prospeo and pipe0

We buy data from Prospeo. It is one of the providers in pipe0's name and
domain work email waterfall, and selectable in the mobile waterfalls, for
the speed and agreement above.

## Who should buy Prospeo

Buy Prospeo if you want cheap, fast, accurate contact data, especially as the
first step before a broader provider, or for lookups where someone is
waiting for the answer.

Look elsewhere if you need the most coverage from one provider or personal
email addresses. For coverage, [Wiza](/reviews/wiza) and
[Surfe](/reviews/surfe) found more in our tests.
