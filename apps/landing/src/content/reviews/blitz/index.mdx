## What is Blitz API?

Blitz API is a B2B data API built around a LinkedIn-based dataset of more
than 550 million contacts. It finds people at companies, enriches LinkedIn
profiles, and returns work emails and, in the United States, mobile numbers.

Its pitch is the price model. Instead of charging per lookup, Blitz sells
three flat monthly plans with unlimited use, subject to a fair use policy.

## Below-average coverage and accuracy

Blitz says it never guesses an email from a name pattern and would rather
return nothing than a risky address. In our benchmark, its data accuracy
was still below average, on par with cheaper providers like LeadMagic.

We ran Blitz on the same 120 LinkedIn profiles as nine other providers. It found
an email for only 45 of the 120, or 38%. Across all 1,204 profiles in our
dataset it found 41%, from 54% in the United States down to 30% in Spain.

For comparison, pipe0's default work email waterfall found an email for 101
of the same 120 people, 84%, and every person Blitz found was among them.
Starting from an email, Blitz found the LinkedIn profile for 10 of 100
people, against 29 for pipe0's reverse lookup waterfall.

## Fast and built for agents

Blitz answered in a median of 0.3 seconds, synchronously. Several contact
providers take seconds per lookup or make you poll for results, so this
matters if an agent or a live form is waiting.

The MCP server exposes one tool per API endpoint and signs in with OAuth,
and the docs ship with an llms.txt index and built-in skills for agents.
Errors name the field that was wrong and the closest valid value.

## What it costs

There are three plans: $399 a month for search and company data, $499 to
add work emails, and $599 to add mobile numbers, which cover the United
States only. A free trial includes 1,000 records, and phone lookups aren't
part of it.

Flat-rate pricing is cheap when you run tens of thousands of lookups a
month. At lower volume, $499 buys fewer emails than paying per result would.
Because Blitz found an email for fewer than half the people we tested, the
real cost per email found is about two and a half times the cost per lookup.

## Blitz API and pipe0

pipe0 competes with Blitz and doesn't use it in its waterfalls. We tested
it on the same inputs as every other provider in our benchmark.

If you already pay for Blitz, the two work together: run Blitz first and
send only the people it misses through pipe0. In our test that kept
coverage at 84% and cut pipe0 spend by about 40%.

## Who should buy Blitz API

Buy Blitz if you enrich tens of thousands of LinkedIn profiles a month,
want answers in under a second, and would rather skip a person than send to
a doubtful address.

Look elsewhere if you need an email for as many people on your list as
possible, mobile numbers outside the United States, or if your volume is
low.
