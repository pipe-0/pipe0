## What is FullEnrich?

FullEnrich does one thing: you give it people, and it gives you their work
emails, personal emails, and mobile numbers. Behind that sits a waterfall of
20+ data providers, Hunter, Wiza, ContactOut, and Snov among them, tried one
after another until one finds an answer. Founded in Paris and now based in
San Francisco, it raised a $2 million seed round in 2025 and sells mostly to
sales teams that want contact data without building anything.

You upload a CSV, connect HubSpot, or call the API. Every email is checked
for syntax, mail server, and mailbox before it comes back, and phone numbers
are checked for landlines and numbers that no longer work.

## Why the waterfall matters

A single database answers from one dataset, and where it is thin you get
nothing. A waterfall asks the next provider instead, so coverage grows with
every source behind it. FullEnrich sells exactly that coverage, packaged so
you never see the providers.

That packaging is the trade-off. You can't tell which provider answered, and
you can't change the order. When a result is wrong, or a provider is slow or
expensive, there is nothing to adjust. We haven't benchmarked FullEnrich, so
we can't say how its hidden order compares with an open one.

## What it costs

Pro plans start at $29 a month for 500 credits and drop to 3.5 cents a credit
at 100,000. A work email costs 1 credit, a personal email 3, and a mobile
number 10. Results that aren't found cost nothing, landlines are free, and
re-enriching the same contact within three months is free too. Unused
credits roll over for three months on monthly plans.

The mobile price is where it adds up. At 10 credits each, a mobile number
costs about 58 cents on the smallest plan and 35 cents at the largest.

## FullEnrich for developers and agents

The API takes batches of up to 100 contacts and sends results back by signed
webhook, typically within 30 to 90 seconds per contact, at 60 requests a
minute. The MCP server at `mcp.fullenrich.com` signs in with OAuth, and
there is a CLI. All of it is included from the free trial up.

## FullEnrich vs pipe0

pipe0 runs waterfalls too, and also bills only for results found. The
differences are what you can see and what else you get. A pipe0 waterfall is
curated and visible: you can see which provider answered and reorder them.
It sits next to company data, 100+ enrichments, and sheets that run on
schedules, and we measure it as about 7x as cost effective as waterfall
providers. Test that on your own list; the
[full comparison](/compare/pipe0-vs-fullenrich) has the details.

## Who should buy FullEnrich

Buy FullEnrich if you need emails and mobile numbers for a list, you want
waterfall coverage without building one, and you don't need to know where
each answer came from.

Look elsewhere if you want to control the waterfall, if mobile numbers at
volume are your biggest cost, or if your needs go beyond contact details.
