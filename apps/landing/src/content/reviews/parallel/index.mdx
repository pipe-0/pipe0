## What is Parallel?

Parallel Web Systems builds web APIs for AI agents. Search returns ranked
excerpts for a stated objective, Extract reads pages, Task answers a
research question with a structured and cited result, FindAll builds
verified lists, and Entity Search finds companies.

## What is Parallel best at?

Researching the rows you already have. Most GTM research is one question
per company: who runs it, where its pricing page is, which tools it uses.
On five company websites, including one with almost no search footprint,
Parallel's Search ranked the right page first on four, in one to three
seconds. The base Task processor answered all five, including a small
company's imprint page at an unguessable path, in 22 to 63 seconds for a
cent each.

Every Task answer carries its sources, the reasoning and a confidence
level, so a person or an agent can check it.

## Which Parallel processor should you use?

Base for research questions; skip lite and the fast search modes for
facts. In our tests lite missed exactly the hard case base solved while
saving little time, and turbo search answered a company revenue question
with the company's acquisition price. Fast modes are for finding pages,
not for reading facts off them.

## Can Parallel build lead lists?

Not well. Task runs returned 17 to 200 local businesses per list and took
eight to fourteen minutes, slower than any other tool we compared. Exa's
agent returned more rows on all four lists in three to six minutes, and
DataForSEO returned 826 to 3,959 in seconds.

Higher processors don't fix it. The ultra processor returned fewer New
York City barbershops than pro, and only 55% of them were in the city.

## What do FindAll and Entity Search cost per row?

They sit at opposite ends. FindAll verifies every match, so its rows are
accurate and expensive: 24 Chicago event venues cost $1.15, about $48 per
1,000. Entity Search returns 81 to 158 companies in two to five seconds
for under a cent, but the rows are company profiles without an address or
a phone number, and it stops well short of large lists.

## Parallel and pipe0

pipe0 runs its web search, page extraction and company entity search on
Parallel, and its research agent uses the Task API for questions that need
a deeper look. New local and company lists go to DataForSEO, the company
databases or Exa instead.

## Parallel, Exa or DataForSEO: which should you use?

Use Parallel to research a page, a fact or a person per company, with a
citation you can check. Use DataForSEO, a company database or Exa to build
the list itself.
