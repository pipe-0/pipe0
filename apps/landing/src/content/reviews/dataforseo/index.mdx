## What is DataForSEO?

DataForSEO is an Estonian data company that sells SEO and marketing data
through APIs: search results, keywords, backlinks and business data.
Agencies and software companies build products on top of it.

Its Business Listings Search API is a database of local businesses with a
map listing. Give it a category, a location and a radius, and it returns
the matching businesses, up to 1,000 per request, each with an address,
phone number, website, rating and opening hours.

## What is the best API for local business lists?

DataForSEO, in our benchmark. On four lists (New York City barbershops,
Dallas–Fort Worth HVAC contractors, Chicago event venues and Miami
property managers) it returned the most businesses every time, two to six
times the next best tool, in seconds. Prompt-based agents from Exa and
Parallel returned hundreds at best, and company databases built from
LinkedIn pages barely list storefront businesses: 26 barbershops in all of
New York City.

## Is DataForSEO business data accurate?

Mostly, and it says when it isn't. Three out of four of its NYC
barbershops matched a shop on the state licence list by address, and every
row was inside a New York City ZIP code. Most of the rest are unlicensed
shops and salons that also cut hair. 8% were listings marked permanently
closed, which one filter removes.

## How fast is DataForSEO, and what does it cost?

A page of 1,000 businesses comes back in one to three seconds and costs
about $0.37. The price is $0.012 per request plus $0.00036 per business,
so you know what a list costs before you run it. Research agents take two
to fourteen minutes per list and bill per run, which puts a short list at
several dollars per 1,000 rows.

## Does DataForSEO find every business?

No source does. Between 42% and 85% of the businesses Exa's agent found
were also in DataForSEO's list; the rest were real misses or businesses
just outside the radius or category. For a complete territory, run a
second source and merge on the address.

## What can't DataForSEO do?

It only knows businesses with a map listing, so online-only companies are
missing entirely. It filters by category, not meaning: "venues that host
corporate events for 100 or more guests" is a category search followed by
a qualifying step on the description, services and attributes each
listing carries. And it has no headcount, revenue or ownership data.

It is also built for developers. Categories are ids such as
`barber_shop`, the location is a latitude, longitude and radius string,
and a failed task comes back inside an HTTP 200 with its own status code.
The free category list and the official MCP server make it easier to use
from an agent than by hand.

## DataForSEO, Exa or a company database: which should you use?

Use DataForSEO when your targets are storefronts, trades, clinics,
restaurants or venues and you need thousands of them with phone numbers.
Use a company database when filters such as headcount or tech stack
describe the list, and Exa when only a prose brief does.
