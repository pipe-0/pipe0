Tech-stack data answers two questions: (1) does this company use a tool,
and (2) what does its stack say about the company as a whole?

Both questions punish wrong answers more than missing ones. Telling a
Shopify store "I saw you're on Magento" ends the conversation.

We looked up 20 websites with [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com) and
[BuiltWith](https://builtwith.com).

Our test dataset consisted of 10 SaaS companies and 10 online stores.

For our test, we used BuiltWith's Domain API and DataForSEO's Domain
Technologies API.

For each website, we checked whether the tool found anything in the
categories that matter for prospecting: CRM and marketing automation, payments, email
and SMS, reviews, support, analytics and ad pixels.

![Tech-stack categories found: SaaS companies BuiltWith 83%, DataForSEO 13%; online stores BuiltWith 76%, DataForSEO 14%.](/media/blog/charts/builtwith-vs-dataforseo-categories.svg)

| | BuiltWith | DataForSEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Categories found, SaaS | 83% | 13% |
| Categories found, online stores | 76% | 14% |
| Lookups failed or empty | 0 of 20 | 2 of 20 |
| Price per lookup | about $0.018 | $0.012 |

## Why DataForSEO falls short

DataForSEO's lookup sees mostly the website framework: Next.js, React,
Shopify, a cookie banner. It found HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo or Stripe
almost nowhere, and it can't look up a website that redirects to another
domain.

## What to watch with BuiltWith

BuiltWith finds the tools, but it never forgets them. It still listed
Magento for two Shopify stores where it last saw Magento in 2019 and 2020,
and HubSpot for companies where it hadn't seen HubSpot in two years.

It also reports entries that aren't technologies at all: popularity
rankings, CDN edge locations, server countries and currencies.

Ask BuiltWith for live technologies only (detected since its latest full
crawl) before you filter on a tool, and drop the non-tool signals.

## Using BuiltWith in pipe0

pipe0's [tech stack pipe](/docs/pipe-catalog/company:techstack:builtwith/2)
does both by default. It cleans up BuiltWith's noisy result set and
returns technologies currently in use, grouped by type.
