Finding companies and people, email addresses, phone numbers, scoring accounts.

No sales without data enrichment. Enriched data has become ubiquitous. So unbiqotous,
that we think that most companies have accepted the frustrations that come with it.

Most companies have stopped investigating it more deeply. They purchase the provider
that promises the highest coverage and accuracy and move on.

As a result, 12 of 15 providers that we've looked at for this post, promise the highest
coverage and "only" verified phone numbers and email addresses.

Yes, the success rate of enrichments of enrichment providers varied from 9% to 86% for single 
providers and was roughly ~96% when using smart waterfalls. In this post, we want to break 
down this difference, why we're seeing it, how good providers manage to be so much better at 
data enrichment and how to pay 9¢ for an enrichment phone enumber instead of 96¢.

We do this by looking at common data enrichment paths. However the techniques here 
apply to most kinds of data enrichment.

## Data enrichment and waterfalls

Let's start at the beginning. Data enrichment is process of extending our existing lists
of companies and people with additional data. You may hand a list of your current users 
to a provider and the provider adds a new colum with your users phone numbers to this list.

Data enrichment is a rather old practice but has gained a lot of attention due to AI.
While scrapers used to be inflexible, AI powered scrapers give us a wide range of new 
possibilities.
