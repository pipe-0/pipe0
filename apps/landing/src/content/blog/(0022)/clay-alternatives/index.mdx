[Clay](https://clay.com) made spreadsheet enrichment mainstream. Teams now
leave it for three reasons: the bill grows faster than the list, tables cap at
50,000 rows, and the agents they already use, like Claude Code or Codex, can't
build in it.

The last reason is the one that will matter most. An agent needs a medium it
can read and change on its own. Clay was built for people clicking, and
data-only APIs hand the agent data but leave the building to you. Keep that in
mind as you read the list.

## pipe0

pipe0 does what Clay does, sheets that enrich people and companies across 50+
providers, and makes all of it something an agent can operate. Ask Claude Code
or ChatGPT a data question and get an answer back. Describe a play, and the
agent builds a sheet you can see, change, and schedule. Sheets hold 2 million
rows, every change can be undone, and the same engine is a
[REST API](/docs) and an [MCP server](/docs/sheets/ai-agents). On average it
runs 6–12x cheaper than Clay. The [pipe0 vs Clay](/compare/pipe0-vs-clay) page
has the side-by-side.

## Apollo.io

![Apollo.io: "Grow revenue with one connected GTM system"](/media/blog/brands/apollo.svg)

Apollo bundles a contact database, sequencing, and a dialer into one per-seat
product. If you want one tool to find and email prospects, it covers more of
the funnel than Clay. Its data comes from one source, Apollo's own database,
so coverage thins outside the US and outside common titles.

## ZoomInfo

![ZoomInfo: "The AI platform for go-to-market teams"](/media/blog/brands/zoominfo.svg)

ZoomInfo is the enterprise incumbent: deep US coverage, org charts, intent
data, and the compliance answers procurement asks for. It's a database, not a
flexible enrichment table, so teams pair it with other tools rather than
replace Clay with it. Reconsidering ZoomInfo itself? See
[ZoomInfo alternatives](/blog/zoominfo-alternatives).

## Four more, quickly

- [FullEnrich](https://fullenrich.com) finds emails and phone numbers across
  many vendors, and does nothing else. A focused swap if contact details are
  all you use Clay for.
- [Ocean.io](https://www.ocean.io) finds lookalikes of companies you've won.
  It complements a Clay-style tool more than it replaces one.
- [Persana AI](https://persana.ai) lets its own agents build and enrich
  lists. It's joining forces with Rox, so check where it's heading.
- [n8n](https://n8n.io) is automation without data. Pair it with an
  enrichment API like pipe0 and skip the provider plumbing.

## When Clay is still the right pick

Clay has the largest template library and community in the category, more
integrations than anyone, and an ecosystem of agencies that know it well. If
your team runs on prebuilt playbooks and clicks through them by hand, stay.
If you want your own agents to build and run the work, need more than 50,000
rows, or want a smaller bill, [try pipe0](https://pipe0.com).
