Tech-stack data answers two questions in outreach: does this company use
the tool I sell against, and what does its stack say about it? Both
punish wrong answers more than missing ones. Telling a Shopify store "I
saw you're on Magento" ends the conversation.

We looked up the same 20 websites, 10 SaaS companies and 10 online stores,
in BuiltWith's Domain API and Amplemarket's company enrichment. For each
website we checked whether the tool found anything in the categories that
matter for prospecting: CRM and marketing automation, payments, email and
SMS, reviews, support, analytics and ad pixels.

## Where Amplemarket falls short

Amplemarket finds about as much as BuiltWith, and more than a website
shows: NetSuite, Okta and Workday next to HubSpot and Klaviyo. But its
list has no dates, so old and current tools look the same. It listed
Magento, OXID and WooCommerce for one store, and Magento, Shopify and
WooCommerce for another. Filtering on "uses Magento" would qualify both.

## What to watch with BuiltWith

BuiltWith finds the tools, but it never forgets them. It still listed
Magento for two Shopify stores where it last saw Magento in 2019 and 2020,
and HubSpot for companies where it hadn't seen HubSpot in two years. It
also reports signals that aren't tools at all: popularity rankings, CDN
edge locations, server countries and currencies.

Ask BuiltWith for live technologies only (detected since its latest full
crawl) before you filter on a tool, and drop the non-tool signals.

BuiltWith counts are live technologies after pipe0's cleanup. Amplemarket's
are its list as returned, without dates, and include generic entries like
"SQL" and "HTTP/3".

## Using BuiltWith in pipe0

pipe0's [tech stack pipe](/docs/pipe-catalog/company:techstack:builtwith/2)
does both by default: it returns technologies currently in use, about 120
recognisable tools per company instead of 400 to 500 entries, grouped by
type. You can widen it to the last 12 months or the full history.
Amplemarket's company enrichment runs in pipe0 too, if you want its
back-office tools next to BuiltWith's live data. The full results are in
our [BuiltWith review](/reviews/builtwith).
